# Branding

The visual identity of Agni and every design decision settled with the owner, in the same commit as the change. The tokens come with the design system (step 5 of `roadmap.md`).

## Brand assets

- **Name**: Agni (« fire » in Bengali and Sanskrit; the god of fire, messenger between the worlds).
- **Logo, favicon, colors, fonts**: none yet (the favicon of the skeleton is SvelteKit's). **[OPEN]** At step 5.
- **Domain**: not reserved yet (`backlog.md`, Waiting on others).

## Principles

- **Never look official**: no « CERFA » in the name or in the interface, no blue-white-red, no Marianne, nothing that could pass for a form of the State.
- **Never name a competitor**, not even in a help text.
- **Mobile first, with a finger, in a boiler room**: large touch targets, high contrast, few words per screen.
- **The PDF is part of the brand**: it is what the client keeps.
