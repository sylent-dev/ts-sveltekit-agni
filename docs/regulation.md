# Regulation

The regulatory frame of the documents Agni produces, each rule with its source and the date it was checked. Read by the steps that touch a certificate (`roadmap.md`, Reference); the module of the mandatory mentions (step 7) cites the section of this note for each one.

A rule is written here only once read in its official text (Légifrance), never from memory nor from a model of a professional body. What is not read yet is marked `**[OPEN]**`.

## The maintenance certificate of a boiler (version 1)

- **Source**: annex 5 of the order of 15 September 2009 on the yearly maintenance of boilers whose nominal power is between 4 and 400 kW, as amended (notably by the order of 24 July 2020). **[OPEN]** The link to the consolidated text on Légifrance, its version and the date of the reading (step 1).
- **Not a CERFA**: its content is set by the annex, not by an official form. Agni builds its own document, which must hold every mention of the annex.
- **Delivery**: handed to the client within 15 days after the visit. **[OPEN]** The exact wording and who keeps it, for how long (the client, the craftsman).
- **Measuring instrument**: the certificate names the brand and the reference of the instrument used for the measures. **[OPEN]** Whether its last calibration must appear too.
- **Mandatory mentions**: **[OPEN]** each one, word for word, with its line of the annex (step 1): the professional, the place and the date of the visit, the identification of the boiler, the measures and their results, the advice and the possible improvements, the signature… The list above is only what to look for, not a rule.
- **Fuels**: gas, fuel oil, wood in version 1. **[OPEN]** What changes in the annex from one fuel to another (the measures, the thresholds).
- **Reference models**: those of UMGCCP/FFB and of the Pro'Réno programme (fillable PDFs). Used only to compare with what Agni produces; never copied (copyright).

## Later

- **Heat pumps**: their maintenance certificate. **[OPEN]** Its text.
- **Refrigerants**: the intervention form CERFA 15497*04, mandatory for categories I to IV, in force since 6 July 2024; a version *05 is announced. **[OPEN]** Whether a CERFA must be reproduced exactly as the official form.
- **BSFF**: the refrigerant tracking slip, through the Trackdéchets API. **[OPEN]** Its terms of use.

## Never

- The VAT certificates 1300-SD and 1301-SD: abolished since 16 February 2025, replaced by a mention on the quote or the invoice.
- The manuals of the manufacturers: copyright.
- A catalog of error codes: not our ground.
